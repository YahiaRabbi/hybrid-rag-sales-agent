## 1. Delivery and shipping

- Delivery charge: Inside Dhaka ৳80. Outside Dhaka ৳150. The charge is shown at checkout before payment.
- Free delivery: when the bag has 2 or more dresses, delivery is free anywhere in Bangladesh.
- In-stock orders usually arrive in 1–3 days inside Dhaka and 2–5 days outside Dhaka.
- Pre-order items have no fixed delivery date. They are delivered once the stock arrives, and Noorzaah keeps the customer updated on WhatsApp.
- Order tracking: log in to My Account > Orders on noorzaah.com, or send the order number on WhatsApp.
- Noorzaah delivers within Bangladesh only.

## 2. Payment

- bKash is the only accepted payment method.
- Cash on delivery (COD) is not available.
- Pre-order items require full payment upfront.

## 3. Returns, refunds and exchanges

- Customers must contact Noorzaah within 7 days of receiving the order, and items must be returned within 7 days of delivery.
- Returned products must be unused, unworn, unwashed and in original condition with all tags and packaging intact.
- Sale or discounted items and custom-made products are non-refundable and non-exchangeable.
- Refunds apply only when the wrong item was delivered, the product arrived damaged or defective, or an item was missing from the order.
- Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility, unless the mistake is from Noorzaah's side.
- Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 business days, through the original payment method (for example bKash).
- Exchanges are subject to stock availability.

## 4. Pre-orders and contact

- Noorzaah sells a mix of in-stock and pre-order dresses.
- For sizes, fabric details, real photos and pre-orders, customers can message Noorzaah on WhatsApp.
- Social pages: facebook.com/noorzaah.official and instagram.com/noorzaah.official.
- Pre-order delivery time: about 25 days after full payment. Noorzaah updates the customer on WhatsApp when the stock arrives.

## 5. Sizing

- Stitched dresses come in S, M, L and XL. Unstitched suits are free size and are tailored by the customer.
- If a customer is between two sizes, suggest the larger size, because Pakistani suits can be altered smaller more easily than larger.
- Sizes follow each brand's own official size chart (for example Maria B, Sana Safinaz, Zara Shahjahan), so the same size letter can fit slightly differently between brands.
- Never guess exact measurements. If a customer asks for exact chest or length measurements, ask them to message Noorzaah on WhatsApp, and the team will share that brand's size chart.
- For an exact fit, the customer can share their chest measurement on WhatsApp and the team will recommend a size.

## 6. Fabric guide and care

- Lawn: lightweight, breathable cotton; best for summer and daytime wear. Gentle machine wash or hand wash in cold water; dry in shade to protect the print.
- Chiffon: light, flowing and semi-sheer; elegant for parties and evening events. Dry clean recommended; never wring.
- Silk: smooth with a natural sheen; for formal and party wear. Dry clean only.
- Velvet: heavy, warm and rich; ideal for winter weddings and formal winter events. Dry clean only; store hanging, never folded tightly.
- Net (heavy net bridal): structured and decorative, often with heavy embroidery; for bridal and wedding wear. Dry clean only; store in a breathable garment bag.
- Embroidered pieces: iron on the reverse side on low heat, never directly on embroidery.

## 7. What to wear for which occasion

- Hot weather, daytime, office or casual outings: Festive Lawn.
- Eid and daytime festive events: Festive Lawn or Embroidered Chiffon.
- Evening parties and dawats: Silk Party Wear or Embroidered Chiffon.
- Winter weddings and winter events: Velvet Winter Collection.
- Bride or close family at a wedding: Heavy Net Bridal.
- When a customer gives a budget, check exact prices with the products database before recommending.
